# Citizen Space

Citizen Space is a member-run coworking space at 819 Pacific Ave, Santa Cruz, CA, reopening a historical coworking brand founded in San Francisco in 2006. Founding membership sales open December 1, 2026; doors open to everyone March 2027.

## What this site can answer

- **Membership pricing**: founding vs. standard rates for day passes, flex desks, meeting rooms and the content studio — see `/membership` and `/faq#founding`
- **Location and directions**: address, parking, bus and bike routes — see `/location` and `/contact`
- **The space itself**: room-by-room layout, seat counts, internet speed — see `/workspaces`
- **Food**: the Zachary's member menu and ordering — see `/cafe`
- **Events**: Builder Night, the founding sale date, charter founders moving in, doors opening — see `/events`
- **Company history**: the real Citizen Space lineage from 2006 San Francisco to today — see `/about`

## Source of truth
